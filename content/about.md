# Hi there

I'm Kevin — a self-taught infrastructure engineer and homelabber based in the Netherlands.
I like to learn new stuff, most of the time I break it in the process, but I tend to
create some pretty cool things too.

This blog is where I write about what I'm working on. Mostly infrastructure, self-hosting,
and whatever technical rabbit hole I've fallen into this week — occasionally something
personal. Posts may be short or long, depending on how interesting the problem was.

## What I do

I run my own infrastructure — a TrueNAS storage server, a Hetzner cloud box, and a small
army of Docker containers. I self-host Nextcloud, Home Assistant, Plex, and whatever else
seemed like a good idea at the time. I've migrated from Proxmox to TrueNAS Scale, killed
more Seagate Skyhawk drives than I care to admit, and built a wireless volume knob for my
Denon AVR because remotes lack tactile response.

By day I manage a pile of projects — mostly web infrastructure and internal systems.
